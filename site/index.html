    Real edge. Not worth waking yet.

    NIRU earns what funding pays: about 7% a year, rich in bull markets and close to nothing when leverage leaves.

    Why it stays buried

    • $10,000 at the start of 2020 became $16,132 by August 2026, 7.4% a year compounded.
    • More capital does not raise the rate. In lean years most of the money sits idle for lack of coins worth holding.
    • 2022 returned +0.2% and 2025 returned -1.3%, with the pool mostly in cash.

    What would wake it

    • Funding back at 2021 levels, when the same rules returned 24%.
    • More venues than Binance, so the idle money finds work.
    • Yield on the cash waiting between positions.
